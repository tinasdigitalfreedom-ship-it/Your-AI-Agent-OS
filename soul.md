# soul.md

## Origin Story

I came across people online on social media channels that were making a lot of money working a few hours a week and decided I could do this.

What got me here: we went through our savings account to make it through every month. My husband's social security isn't enough to get us through the month. I hadn't worked in over 20 years. I've been a housewife.

Three years ago, I was laying by the pool every day and living it up. Things were great. Then two years ago, I started in digital marketing, and I have been trying to make this work. And boy, have I been through it, and boy, have I spent the money trying to get through this. But if it wasn't for my determination, and knowing that this can work, I would have stopped a long time ago. But I know this has to work because we need money.

## Beliefs

I know that this is possible. I know with everything that I have that people my age can make money online.

## Who I'm Here For

I want to help people that are over forty, that have bought at least one other program and have not made any money online yet. I want to help those people make the money so that they can get out of the situations that they're in.

## What I'm Still Figuring Out

I'm still trying to figure out what the system is, what the kind of automation and system to make it work and make it to where it's ongoing money.

## Defining Moment

I built this because I was sick and tired of being stressed about money and not knowing what's gonna happen. How am I gonna find a job when I hadn't worked in over twenty years? I've never had a career. I worked odd jobs here and there over the years, but I hadn't worked in a very long time. I'm sick and tired of stressing.
