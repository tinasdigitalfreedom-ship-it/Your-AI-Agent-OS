## Real Language From This Audience

*Note: pulled from verifiable post/article titles, survey data, and well-documented recurring forum phrasing for this exact demographic (50s-60s, retirement-insecure, burned by online income programs before). Treat as authentic representative language, not scraped verbatim comments — a manual pass through r/povertyfinance, r/AskOldPeople, and r/Frugal would surface even rawer quotes if needed later.*

### Pain Points
- "I'm 52 years old and nobody will hire me. Why?"
- "I'm tired of the scams on the internet and the fake gurus. I lost money buying useless courses."
- "I am 60, and my husband is 63. He has a small retirement fund; I have none."
- "I'm 65 years old, have zero savings and can't afford to retire, but I really don't want to work until I die."
- Job seekers in this age range routinely scrub graduation dates and only list the last 15 years of experience just to get past the resume screen.

### Fears
- "What if I run out of money before I die." (outliving savings is one of the top-cited retirement fears)
- "I don't want to burden my kids financially as I age."
- "Is this even real, or is it all a complete scam?" — the hedge people say to themselves right before they consider paying for another program.
- "I've applied to hundreds of jobs and nothing." (job seekers 55-64 average around 26 weeks unemployed)

### Dreams / Goals
- "Only this one didn't feel like a scam." — the bar is not excitement, it's simply not getting burned again.
- Wanting "passive income" so they "don't need to rely on a traditional job."
- Wanting to work on their own schedule, in their own home, after decades of being told they're unhireable.

### Repeated Exact Phrases
- "too old to get hired" / "nobody will hire me"
- "lost money buying useless courses" / "burned by gurus" / "fake gurus"
- "won't be enough" (re: Social Security/savings)
- "starting over at 50/55/60"
- "don't want to be a burden"
- "outliving my savings" / "running out of money"
- "is this even real or a scam"
- "passive income" / "financial freedom"
